# About Prime Safe
Prime Safe is a privacy-focused application designed to help users store, organize, and manage their personal files in one convenient place. It provides a dedicated space for private photos, videos, documents, and notes, bringing essential file-management features together in a simple and user-friendly interface. The application is designed for people who want better control over their personal content without dealing with complicated file-management processes. Whether you need to keep important documents, save personal notes, or organize photos and videos, Prime Safe helps you manage your content within a dedicated vault.

# Built for Privacy and Convenience
Prime Safe combines a clean user experience with privacy-focused features. Its vault system helps separate personal content from everyday files, while its simple navigation makes it easier to access and manage stored items.

# What You Can Do with Prime Safe
- Photo Storage:- Keep personal photos organized inside your private vault.
- Video Storage:- Store and manage videos in a dedicated section.
- Document Management:- Keep supported documents, including PDFs and text files, together in one place.
- Private Notes:- Create and store personal notes within the application.
- Vault Access:- Use the app's access-control features to help restrict access to private content.
- Organized Interface:- Navigate between different content categories without unnecessary complexity.

# Designed for Everyday Use
Prime Safe focuses on making personal file management straightforward. Instead of keeping private content scattered across different folders and applications, users can bring supported files together in a single, organized space. From everyday photos to important documents and personal notes, Prime Safe offers a convenient way to manage digital content while keeping privacy in mind.
